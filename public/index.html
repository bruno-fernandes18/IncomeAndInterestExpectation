CLEAR ASSUMPTIONS

How it's calculated

Monthly rhythm. Existing savings earn interest first, then that month's contribution is added. This means the first deposit earns for all remaining months and the final deposit earns no interest.

Annual rates. An annual rate is treated as an effective rate and converted to its equivalent monthly rate before compounding.

Growth percentage. Estimated interest is compared with total contributions. The starting balance is zero, so a percentage increase from the starting balance is undefined.

For illustration. Projections do not include fees, taxes, inflation, or changing returns. They are estimates, not guarantees.
