How it's calculated

A monthly investing rhythm, with each deposit earning interest only after it is added.

01

Start at zero

The projection begins with a R$0.00 balance before the first contribution.

02

Compound the existing balance

At the start of each month, the invested balance earns the monthly interest rate.

03

Add the new contribution

The monthly contribution is added after interest. The first deposit earns for all remaining months, and the final deposit earns no interest.

04

Convert annual rates

An effective annual interest rate is converted to its equivalent monthly rate before compounding. A yearly income amount is split into twelve monthly contributions.

Monthly balance = previous balance × (1 + monthly rate) + monthly contribution

Growth percentage compares estimated interest with total contributions. Estimates exclude taxes, fees, inflation, and changing returns.
