THE MATHEMATICS

How it's calculated

A monthly contribution meets a rate that credits interest at its chosen pace.

01

Keep each deposit in sequence

Monthly income is added after an interest tick, so the newest deposit waits until the next one.

Monthly depositCm = Ca / 12An annual income input is divided into twelve monthly deposits.
02

Choose the interest rhythm

A monthly rate compounds every month. An annual rate compounds every twelve months.

Interest ticks per yearr = x / 100p = 1 or p = 12x is the entered percentage, r is its decimal form; p = 1 for monthly rates and p = 12 for annual rates.
03

Apply interest at each tick

The balance grows at the selected rate tick, then this month's deposit is added.

Balance after month mBm = Bm−1(1 + r) + CIf m is not an interest tick, Bm = Bm−1 + C. The last deposit earns no future tick.
04

Count each later tick

Every deposit compounds only for the interest ticks that happen after it.

Balance after N monthsBN = C Σm=1N(1 + r)q(m)q(m) counts the later interest ticks; a final deposit has q(N) = 0.
